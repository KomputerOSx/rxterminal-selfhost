This statement describes how {{organization_name}} protects personal data processed through this system.

1. Who Is Responsible

{{organization_name}} ({{organization_address}}) is the Data Controller for the personal data collected through this portal and the staff system behind it. Data protection contact: {{contact_email}}.

The software is {{app_name}}, open-source software run on infrastructure chosen and controlled by {{organization_name}}. Its authors do not host, receive or access your data.

2. What Personal Data Is Processed

  • Customers: name, date of birth, postcode, email address (optional), the service requested, and queue and appointment history.
  • Staff: name, email address, role and sign-in activity.
  • Health information: only what a customer chooses to provide when describing a request.

3. Lawful Basis

  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): managing queues and appointments.
  • Public task (Art. 6(1)(e)): where the service is publicly funded.
  • Health or social care (Art. 9(2)(h)): for any health-related information provided.
  • Contract and legal obligation: for staff accounts and required records.

4. Retention

  • Customer identifying details are anonymised automatically after {{pii_retention_days}} days.
  • Anonymised records may be kept for statistics and regulatory purposes.
  • Staff account data is kept while the account is active and removed on request when the account is deleted.
  • Audit logs are kept according to {{organization_name}}'s retention setting.

5. Where Data Is Stored And Who Processes It

Data is stored on the servers operated by or for {{organization_name}}. Any hosting, email or backup providers used are engaged by {{organization_name}} under appropriate data protection terms. Contact {{contact_email}} for the current list.

6. Your Rights

You can ask to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to the use of your data, or to receive it in a portable format. Contact {{contact_email}}; we respond within one calendar month.

7. Security

  • Patient identifiers are encrypted at rest in the database.
  • Staff access is role-based, with optional two-factor authentication and an audit trail.
  • Connections are encrypted with HTTPS when the site is configured for it.
  • Access to the underlying server and backups is limited to authorised people.

8. Data Breaches

If a personal data breach occurs, {{organization_name}} will assess the risk without delay, notify the supervisory authority within 72 hours where required, and tell affected individuals when there is a high risk to them.

9. Complaints

Contact {{contact_email}} first. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk, or to your local data protection authority if you are outside the UK.
