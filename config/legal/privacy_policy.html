2. What Information Is Collected

When you book an appointment or join the queue through this portal, the following may be collected:

  • Your first and last name
  • Date of birth (used to identify you at the counter)
  • Postcode (used to confirm you are registered with this organisation)
  • Your email address, if you ask for a confirmation or reminder
  • The service you are requesting

Payment card details are never stored by this portal.

3. Why Your Information Is Collected (Lawful Basis)

Your information is processed under UK GDPR Article 6:

  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)): to manage appointments and the queue efficiently and give you timely service.
  • Public task (Article 6(1)(e)): where the service is a publicly funded health service.

If you provide health-related information, the organisation also processes it under Article 9(2)(h): provision of health or social care.

4. How Your Information Is Used

  • To place you in the queue or confirm your appointment
  • To let staff prepare for your visit before you reach the counter
  • To send you the confirmation or reminder you asked for
  • To keep the records the organisation is required to keep

5. How Long Your Information Is Kept

Personal details such as your name, date of birth and postcode are automatically anonymised after {{pii_retention_days}} days. Anonymised records cannot be linked back to you and may be kept for statistics and regulatory purposes.

6. Who Your Information Is Shared With

Your information is visible to authorised staff of {{organization_name}} only. It is not sold and is not used for marketing by third parties.

{{organization_name}} may use service providers to run this system, for example hosting and email delivery. They act only on the organisation's instructions.

7. Your Rights

  • Access: ask for a copy of your personal data.
  • Rectification: ask for inaccurate data to be corrected.
  • Erasure: ask for your data to be deleted.
  • Restriction and objection: ask the organisation to limit or stop using your data.
  • Portability: ask for your data in a portable format.

To use any of these rights, speak to a member of staff or email {{contact_email}}. The organisation will respond within one calendar month.

If you are unhappy with how your data is handled you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113. If you are outside the UK, contact your local data protection authority.
