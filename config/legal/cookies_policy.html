1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They let the site remember your session and preferences.

2. Cookies We Use

This site uses only the cookies it needs to work. They do not track you across other websites.

3. Managing Cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking the cookies above will stop parts of the site, such as booking and sign-in, from working.
