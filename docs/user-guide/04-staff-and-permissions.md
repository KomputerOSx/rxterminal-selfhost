# 4. Staff and Permissions

## Roles

Every staff member has one role in your organization:

- **Owner**, full control: billing, organization name, and promoting or demoting anyone. An organization always has at least one owner.
- **Admin**, day-to-day management: queue, appointments, services, calendars, upsells, devices, kiosk, analytics, audit logs, and most organization settings. Can invite and remove staff, but can't promote, demote, or manage billing or the organization name.
- **User**, queue and appointment work only.

## Inviting staff

1. Go to Members and enter the person's email address in the invite field.
2. Click Invite.

They receive an email with a link to join. New invitations always join as a user. Promote them afterward if needed. On the Starter plan, you can't invite anyone while you're at your member limit.

## Promoting and demoting

Only an owner can do this. Click the edit icon next to a member and pick User or Admin. The change applies immediately.

## Changing ownership

1. To promote a member to owner, click the edit icon next to their name, then Promote to Owner. Type their email address to confirm.
2. RxTerminal sends a confirmation code to your own inbox. Enter it to complete the promotion.
3. To demote an owner to admin, click Demote to Admin next to their name and type their email address.
4. RxTerminal sends a confirmation code to the owner being demoted. They must confirm it themselves, so ownership can't be taken away without their knowledge.

An owner can't be removed or demoted directly to a lower role. Demote them to admin first, then remove them if needed. An organization must always keep at least one owner.

## Removing staff or leaving

- Remove a member from the Members page.
- Leave an organization yourself from Settings. If you're the sole owner and other members remain, promote a co-owner first.

## Switching organizations

If you belong to more than one organization, use the organization menu at the top of the page to switch. Your role can differ between organizations.
