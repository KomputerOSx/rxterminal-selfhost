# 11. Analytics

Go to Analytics to see how your queue is performing. Choose a period: today, this week, this month, last month, or all time.

## What you'll see

- **Totals**: total requests, completed, cancelled, and completion rate.
- **Requests by service**: which services get the most requests.
- **Request type**: walk-in versus appointment.
- **Busiest days**: requests by day of the week.
- **Activity heatmap**: requests by day and hour.
- **Busiest hours**: requests by hour of the day.

Use this to spot busy periods and staff accordingly.
