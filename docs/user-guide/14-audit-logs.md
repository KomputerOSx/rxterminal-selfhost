# 14. Audit Logs

Audit Logs record staff and system actions in your organization: members added or removed, roles changed, services and appointments created or edited, openings and blocked dates added or removed, settings updated, and more.

## Viewing logs

Go to Audit Logs. Requires the audit logs permission. By default, you'll see the last 7 days. Older entries are available going back as far as your plan's retention: 7 days on Starter, unlimited on Business.

- Filter by date range, user, event type, or resource.
- Use Extensive Search to search everything, including IP addresses and full event detail.
- Click an entry to see its full detail, along with other recent activity from the same user.

Use audit logs to review what changed and who made the change.
