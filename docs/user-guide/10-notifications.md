# 10. Notifications

RxTerminal sends you notifications for important account and platform updates. Find them using the bell icon at the top of the page.

## Managing your inbox

- Click View to open a notification's link, or Mark Read to clear it without opening it.
- Use Mark All Read to clear the unread count in one step.
- Click Delete to remove a notification from your inbox permanently. There's no undo.

The unread badge on the bell icon updates as you read or delete notifications.
