# 2. Organization Setup

## Settings

Settings holds your organization's profile: name, slug, contact details, address, logo, and the public URLs customers use to reach you. Opening hours, appointment booking, custom domain, portal appearance, features, kiosk, and devices are each their own page in the sidebar, not part of Settings.

## Organization profile

- **Name**, shown throughout the app and to customers. Only an owner can change it.
- **Slug**, the part of your public booking URL after the domain. Must be unique across RxTerminal. You can change it once every 30 days. Links and QR codes using the old slug stop working.
- **Postcode region**, used to validate customer postcodes.
- **Service price currency**, the currency your service prices are shown in to customers.
- **Time zone**, used for your queues and appointments.
- **Contact email and phone**, shown to customers on your public portal.
- **Address**, shown to customers on your public portal.
- **Collect postcode on kiosk and walk-in forms**, turn off if you don't need a customer's postcode.
- **Collect date of birth on kiosk and walk-in forms**, turn off if you don't need a customer's date of birth. Customers are then looked up by name or booking reference only, and upsell rules can no longer target by age or visit type. See Upsells.
- **Logo**, shown on the kiosk, the public portal, and portal printouts. See Logo below.

Everything except the name can be changed by an owner or admin. Settings also shows your portal URL and, if you have one, your custom domain, and lets you leave the organization.

## Logo

Your active logo appears on the kiosk, the public portal, and portal printouts. Every logo is reviewed by the RxTerminal team before you can use it, to stop inappropriate images appearing on your public pages.

1. In Settings, under Upload logos, choose up to 3 images. Each must be a PNG, JPEG, GIF, or WebP file under 5 MB.
2. Click Upload for review. Each logo appears under Your submissions as Pending review.
3. When a logo is approved, you get a notification. Click Use this logo on it to make it your active logo.

You can have up to 3 logos pending review or approved at a time. Declined logos don't count, and removing a submission frees up its place.

Almost all logos are approved. Review can take up to 48 hours. If it's taking longer, contact support at support@strixon.co.uk.

If a logo is declined, you get a notification with the reason, which is also shown under the logo. Upload a new one that fixes the problem.

To stop showing a logo, click Remove active logo. Until you choose another one, the kiosk and portal show an icon instead. You can pick the icon in Settings.

## Opening hours

Set open and close times for each day of the week. Closed days can be left blank. Opening hours don't control appointment slots. Appointment Booking does. See Appointments.

## Feature toggles

The Features page lists optional capabilities:

- **Public booking portal** is always on. Every other feature below needs it.
- **Online appointment booking**, lets customers book appointments through your public portal.
- **Mobile service request**, lets customers join the queue from their phone by scanning your in-store QR code.
- **Online appointment check-in**, lets customers check in for a booked appointment from their phone.
- **Waiting time**, shows average wait time on the queue screen and waiting display.

Some features require your plan to include appointments. See Billing.

## Custom domain

Available on the Business plan. To use your own domain instead of the default RxTerminal URL:

1. Go to Custom Domain in the sidebar.
2. Enter your domain.
3. Add the DNS record RxTerminal shows you at your domain provider.
4. Use the status check on the page to confirm the domain has verified.

## Portal appearance

Choose the theme customers see on your public booking portal, or pick your own colors.
