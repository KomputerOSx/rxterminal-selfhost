# 2. Organization Setup

## Settings

Settings holds your organization's profile: name, slug, contact details, address, logo, and the public URLs customers use to reach you. Opening hours, appointment booking, portal appearance, features, kiosk, and devices are each their own page in the sidebar, not part of Settings.

## Organization profile

- **Name**, shown throughout the app and to customers. Only an owner can change it.
- **Slug**, the part of your public booking URL after the domain. Must be unique on this server. You can change it at any time, but links and QR codes using the old slug stop working.
- **Postcode region**, used to validate customer postcodes.
- **Service price currency**, the currency your service prices are shown in to customers.
- **Time zone**, used for your queues and appointments.
- **Contact email and phone**, shown to customers on your public portal.
- **Address**, shown to customers on your public portal.
- **Collect postcode on kiosk and walk-in forms**, turn off if you don't need a customer's postcode.
- **Collect date of birth on kiosk and walk-in forms**, turn off if you don't need a customer's date of birth. Customers are then looked up by name or booking reference only, and upsell rules can no longer target by age or visit type. See Upsells.
- **Logo**, shown on the kiosk, the public portal, and portal printouts. See Logo below.

Everything except the name can be changed by an owner or admin. Settings also shows your portal URL and lets you leave the organization.

## Logo

Your logo appears on the kiosk, the public portal, and portal printouts.

1. In Settings, under Organization logo, click Choose file and pick a PNG, JPEG, GIF, or WebP image under 5 MB.
2. Click Upload. The new logo is used straight away and replaces the old one.

To stop showing a logo, click Remove logo. Until you upload another, the kiosk and portal show an icon instead. You can pick the icon in Settings.

## Opening hours

Set open and close times for each day of the week. Closed days can be left blank. Opening hours don't control appointment slots. Appointment Booking does. See Appointments.

## Feature toggles

The Features page lists optional capabilities:

- **Public booking portal** is always on. Every other feature below needs it.
- **Online appointment booking**, lets customers book appointments through your public portal.
- **Mobile service request**, lets customers join the queue from their phone by scanning your in-store QR code.
- **Online appointment check-in**, lets customers check in for a booked appointment from their phone.
- **Waiting time**, shows average wait time on the queue screen and waiting display.

## Portal appearance

Choose the theme customers see on your public booking portal, or pick your own colors.
