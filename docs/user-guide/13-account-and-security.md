# 13. Account and Security

## Profile

Go to Profile to update your name and email address. Changing your email requires confirming both your old and new addresses.

## Two-factor authentication

You need a password set on your account before you can turn on two-factor authentication.

1. Go to Profile, find Authenticator App (TOTP), and choose Set Up.
2. Scan the code with an authenticator app.
3. Enter the code it generates to confirm setup.

Once enabled, you'll be asked for a code at login. If you lose access to your authenticator, use the email fallback option on the login screen. To turn it off, choose Disable and enter your current password. This signs you out of all your other sessions.

## Email preferences

Choose which emails you receive from your Profile page.

## Deleting your account

1. Go to Profile and choose Delete Account.
2. Type your email address to confirm, and enter the confirmation code sent to your inbox.

You can't delete your account while you're the sole owner of an organization that still has other members, or that has an active subscription. Promote a co-owner, remove other members, or cancel the subscription first.
