# 6. Appointments

Appointments require the Business plan. See Billing.

## Calendars

Calendars group services for scheduling. Every organization starts with a default calendar, which can't be archived.

1. Go to Calendars to create, rename, or recolor a calendar.
2. Archive a calendar you no longer use. Its services, openings, blocked dates, and any future booked or checked-in appointments on it, move to the default calendar automatically.

## Booking an appointment

1. Go to Appointments and click New Appointment.
2. Choose the service, date, and an available time slot.
3. Enter the customer's details.
4. Save. A confirmation email is sent if you entered an email address.

Available slots are based on the service's duration and your appointment booking settings. If the date is blocked, you'll see the reason instead of slots.

## Viewing appointments

Use the Day, Week, and Month buttons on the Appointments page to switch views.

- **Day** lists every appointment for the chosen date, with actions like check in.
- **Week** shows Monday to Sunday side by side. Click an appointment to open it, or a day's heading to open that day.
- **Month** shows how many appointments are booked, checked in, cancelled, or no-show on each day. Click a day to open it.

Use the arrows to move back or forward, Today to jump to today, or pick a date and click Go. The totals next to the arrows cover the whole day, week, or month you're viewing. Closed dates are shaded with their reason.

If you have more than one calendar, choose a calendar to see only its appointments. Print uses the same calendar.

## Managing appointments

- **Check in** a customer on the day of their appointment to add them to the queue. Only today's booked appointments can be checked in.
- **Return to booked** undoes a check-in, if you checked in the wrong appointment.
- **Cancel** a booked appointment. A cancellation email is sent if an email address is on file.
- **Mark no-show** if the customer didn't arrive.
- **Resend confirmation** to email the customer their booking details again.
- **Search** by name or booking reference to find an appointment quickly.
- **Print** the day's appointment list, optionally filtered by service or calendar.

## Appointment booking settings

Go to Appointment Booking in the sidebar. These settings control when customers can book on the public portal, at the kiosk, and when staff book. They are separate from your opening hours.

## Booking mode

Choose one:

- **Rolling dates**: your weekly bookable windows repeat every week, up to the booking window. Use this for regular appointments.
- **Set openings**: instead of a booking window, you add openings. Each opening has a start date, an end date, and its own weekly bookable windows. Use this for seasonal clinics or set booking periods.

Click Save appointment booking after changing the mode.

## Rolling dates

- **Booking window (days ahead)** sets how far ahead customers can book.
- **Bookable windows** set up to 3 time windows for each day of the week. Turn a day off to stop bookings on it.

## Openings

In Set openings mode, customers can only book inside an opening.

1. Under Openings, click New opening.
2. Add a Name, such as "Flu season".
3. Choose the Start date and End date.
4. If you have more than one calendar, choose All calendars, or choose Selected calendars and tick the ones it applies to.
5. Under Bookable windows, turn on each day you take bookings and set up to 3 time windows for it. New openings start with a copy of your rolling bookable windows.
6. Click Save opening.

If a calendar has its own opening on a day, customers booking that calendar's services see only its windows. Otherwise they see the All calendars opening.

Openings for the same calendar can't overlap on the same weekday. To change an opening, click the edit icon on its row. To remove it, click the delete icon.

## Blocked dates

Blocked dates stop bookings on days you're closed, such as bank holidays. They apply in both booking modes, including inside openings.

To block a date:

1. Under Add blocked date, choose a Start date.
2. To block several days, choose an End date. Leave it blank for a single day.
3. Add a Reason. Customers see it when they hover over the date.
4. If you have more than one calendar, choose All calendars, or choose Selected calendars and tick the ones it applies to.
5. Click Add blocked date.

To import public holidays:

1. Under Import public holidays, choose your Country or region.
2. If you have more than one calendar, choose All calendars, or choose Selected calendars and tick the ones it applies to.
3. Click Import public holidays.

This adds the public holidays for the next 12 months. Dates you've already blocked are skipped. Run it again each year to add the next year's holidays.

To remove a blocked date, click the delete icon on its row in the Blocked dates table.

## Appointments to review

Blocking a date, removing an opening, or changing your settings never cancels existing appointments. If booked appointments no longer fit your settings, you'll see a warning, and they're listed under Appointments to review. Open each one to reschedule or cancel it.
