# 9. Public Booking Portal

Your public booking portal is a page customers can visit directly, at your slug's URL or your custom domain. It's always on. Go to Features in the sidebar to turn on the capabilities below.

## What customers can do

- **Browse services** that require an appointment.
- **Book an appointment**, if Online appointment booking is on. They pick a service, date, and time slot, then receive a confirmation email with a calendar file attached. Dates they can't book are greyed out. Hovering over a blocked date shows its reason.
- **Join the queue from their phone**, if Mobile service request is on. They scan your in-store QR code and submit a walk-in request without approaching a staff member.
- **Check in for a booked appointment**, if Online appointment check-in is on. They confirm by booking reference, or by last name and date of birth.
- **See your opening times**, if Opening times page is on. It shows your weekly hours and whether you're open now.
- **Contact you**, if Contact page is on. It shows your address with a map link, your phone number and your email. They contact you directly by phone or email.

## QR codes

If you run a staff waiting display (a TV or tablet showing the live queue), it shows a QR code customers can scan to reach your portal. It automatically rotates every hour, so a photo of it stops working within the hour. The QR code doesn't appear at all if none of the three capabilities above are on.

The QR's destination and wording adjust automatically to whichever portal features you have on: joining the queue, checking in for an appointment, booking one, or any combination of them.
