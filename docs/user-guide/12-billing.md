# 12. Billing

## Plans

- **Starter**: 1 staff member, up to 3 services, 1 upsell rule, 200 requests a month, 7 days of audit log history. No appointments, calendars, kiosk, or custom domain.
- **Business**: unlimited staff, services, upsell rules, and monthly requests, full audit log history, and access to appointments, calendars, kiosk, and custom domains.

## Free trial

Your first organization gets a 30-day free trial on the Business plan. No card required. Subscribe to an available plan before the trial ends to keep access, or end the trial early from the Billing page.

## Subscribing

1. Go to Billing and choose a plan under Choose a Plan (or Change Plan, if you're on a trial).
2. Enter your payment details through Stripe Checkout.
3. Your plan is active as soon as checkout completes.

## Changing plans

- **Upgrading** takes effect immediately. You're charged a prorated amount for the rest of the current billing period.
- **Downgrading** takes effect at the start of your next billing date. You keep your current plan's features until then.
- If a downgrade to Starter would put you over its limits (for example, too many services, staff, or upsell rules), you're walked through a wizard to bring your organization within those limits first.

## Cancelling

Choose Cancel Subscription on the Billing page. You keep access until the end of your current billing period, and can resume any time before then.

## Promo codes

1. Enter a code on the Billing page and apply it.
2. If you're not yet subscribed, it's used automatically at checkout.
3. If you already have a subscription, it applies to your next bill right away.
4. Remove a code from the same page at any time.

## Payment method and invoices

Choose Manage in Stripe to open the Stripe billing portal, where you can update your payment method and download past invoices. Recent charges are also listed directly on the Billing page.

## Currency

RxTerminal bills in GBP.
